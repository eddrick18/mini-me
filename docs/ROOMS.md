# Rooms and activity environments

Home uses a finished cozy room. Studying uses a reading nook, Working a workspace, Gym a neighborhood gym, Running a park trail, and Music an evening lounge. The user's approved backgrounds were revised into larger, sharper pixel clusters while retaining their composition. Production files are assets/world/*-pixel.png; earlier draft images are retained but not referenced by the app. Built-in image generation was used, with source prompts recorded in WORLD-ART.md. Figma and Rive synchronization is not included.

WorldBackdrop owns image sizing, environment labels, background failure/retry and subtle ambient motes. Images fill their explicitly constrained container using cover, preserving proportions; phone-width views crop the far edges. SpriteScene retains the 238dp stage and common feet baseline with a centered, unscaled-by-background sprite slot. Scenery is behind the character, with a clear center. Ambient motion follows the existing foreground, still-pose and OS reduced-motion policy.

Your room is a visual inventory with search, category filters, selected/equipped/locked states and six decorations. Oak desk, leafy plant and island print are available at level 1; sunshine blooms at level 2; ube desk and night print at level 3. One item fits each fixed slot: desk at left floor, plant at right floor, print at right wall. These positions keep the character clear. DecorationArt extends the existing pixel SVG icon approach and renders both inventory thumbnails and placed items.

Tapping previews a draft without persistence. Locked items can be previewed but cannot be saved. Save room uses the existing domain command/store and confirms after commit. Clear slot/all changes only the draft; Cancel discards it. Appearance, activities, XP and quest receipts are unaffected. Home and wardrobe show the equipped room; activity environments are automatic and do not carry its decorations.

Legacy ledgers gain an empty room without reset. Backup format version 3 includes room choices and quest receipts; versions 1 and 2 remain importable. Slot IDs and level ownership are validated in persistence and import. Older builds reject newer backups instead of dropping room choices. The import preview names the incoming decorations.
